Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SynthFit (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the SynthFit web and Android applications. SynthFit is a guided yoga practice tool with optional wearable heart-rate during practice that works offline by default.

Summary

Practice data stays on your device unless you choose optional Google Sign-In to sync it into files in your own Google Drive (a YogApp folder). Optional community features (teacher listings and blogs) store the profile content you submit on SynthFit’s community service so other visitors can see approved listings. Sign-in and Drive access are provided by Google; Google’s handling of your Google Account is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. On the website we keep a simple anonymous tally of which pages were visited (see below). (Google Privacy Policy).

Visitor counts

The SynthFit website uses GoatCounter to count visits to pages such as Routines, Learn, Privacy, and Settings. Counts are anonymous aggregates (no cookies, no accounts, no advertising profiles). GoatCounter’s own practices are described in their privacy policy. (GoatCounter, privacy policy).

Information we do not collect

We do not use advertising networks or tracking pixels. Outside optional Google Sign-In / Drive sync and optional community teacher or blog content you choose to publish, we do not run a general SynthFit account database of your practice history.

Community teachers and blogs

If you register as a teacher or publish a community blog post while signed in with Google, SynthFit stores that content on a community service (cloud resolver / Firestore) so it can be reviewed and, when approved, shown publicly.

  • Teacher listings may include your Google sign-in email (used as the listing key), display name, short blurb, profile photo, website URL, optional contact email or phone, country/region, YouTube links, approval/disabled status, and — if an admin bans the listing — a ban flag and reason. Blog posts include title, summary, body, image, author email, publish status, and moderation flags.
  • We use this information to show approved teachers on the Teachers page, show published blog posts, let you edit your own listing or drafts, and let site admins approve, disable, ban, or delete listings and posts. Ban reasons are shown to the banned teacher in the App.
  • Community teacher and blog data remains until you ask for deletion (or an admin deletes it), or until you clear related content through admin tools. Practice data in Google Drive is separate and is not copied into the community store.

Information stored on your device

To provide the App’s features, SynthFit stores data locally on your device (for example in the browser’s IndexedDB or the Android app’s local storage), including:

  • Preferences (hold times, music, reminders, accessibility options, and optional body profile details such as date of birth, weight, and height)
  • Practice session history and streak statistics
  • Custom routines you create
  • Optional wearable history (heart rate, steps, sleep, recovery metrics, blood pressure, SpO₂, weight, body fat) from Bluetooth devices you link, and optional Health Connect import/export on Android
  • Optional food diary entries and meal photos (synced to yogapp-food.json in your Drive when signed in)

This data remains on your device (and in your Drive sync files when enabled). Coaching health snapshots are not stored by SynthFit; trainers only read a Drive file you share. You can remove local data by clearing the App’s site data in your browser, or by clearing/uninstalling the Android app.

Android Health Connect

On Android, you can optionally enable Health Connect sync. When enabled, SynthFit may read steps, workouts, sleep, historical heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen (SpO₂), weight, and body fat that other apps have written to Health Connect on your device, and may write yoga sessions, bike rides, day step totals from a linked Hume Band, cuff blood-pressure readings, and band SpO₂ readings back to Health Connect.

Health Connect sharing is on-device and user-controlled: other apps see data only if you grant them Health Connect permissions. SynthFit does not upload Health Connect data to its own servers. You can disable sync in Wearable settings and revoke permissions in Android Settings → Health Connect.

Health Connect does not provide live heart rate during practice. Live BPM still uses Bluetooth Low Energy.

Google Sign-In and Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, SynthFit uses Google Identity Services / OAuth to request the following scopes:

  • openid, email, and profile — so we can show your name, email address, and profile picture in the App’s profile UI
  • drive.file — so the App can create and update sync files it creates in your Google Drive (typically under a YogApp folder; not your whole Drive)
  • youtube.readonly — optional; requested only when you connect YouTube playlists for background music, so the App can list playlists you own and their videos (it does not modify your YouTube account)

What Google user data we access

  • Basic profile information from Google (name, email, and profile picture URL) via Google’s userinfo endpoint
  • JSON sync files in a YogApp folder in your Drive containing your preferences, session history, stats, custom routines, optional food diary entries, and optional Hume vitals data. When you opt in to coaching health sharing, a separate dedicated health-share file may be shared with your trainer’s Google account as a reader only.
  • When you opt in: titles and video IDs of YouTube playlists you own, used only to play background music during practice

How we use Google user data

We use this information only to:

  • Identify you in the App while you are signed in
  • Sync your SynthFit practice data across devices using files the App creates in your Google Drive
  • When you enable coaching health scopes, grant your trainer reader access to a dedicated health-share file you own (not your full practice or wearable sync files)
  • Play videos from YouTube playlists you choose as optional practice background music (via YouTube’s embed player)

We do not use Google user data for advertising, selling data, credit decisions, or training generalized AI/ML models. SynthFit does not store coaching health payloads on its own servers.

How we store Google user data

  • Profile display information (name, email, picture URL) is kept in memory for the current session while you are signed in
  • Practice data is stored on your device and, when sync is enabled, written only to App-created files in your Google Drive under your Google Account (in the YogApp folder)
  • Access tokens from Google are held in memory for API calls and are not uploaded to any SynthFit server (there is none)

Sharing, transfer, and disclosure

SynthFit does not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties. Sync writes go only to Google Drive APIs acting on your account. Optional coaching health sharing uses Google Drive file permissions you grant to a trainer’s Google email. Optional Android Health Connect read/write stays on your device and is shared only with other apps you authorize in Health Connect. Google may process data according to its own policies when you use Google Sign-In and Drive.

Data protection

Communication with Google APIs and the community service uses HTTPS. Local practice data is stored in your device’s browser or app storage. Community teacher and blog content you submit is stored on SynthFit’s community service for moderation and public display when approved.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you clear App/site storage or uninstall the app
  • Drive sync files remain in your Google Account until you delete the YogApp folder/files (or revoke access via Google Account settings)
  • Optional teacher listings and blog posts remain on the community service until deleted by you (where the App allows) or by an admin, or until you contact us to request removal
  • You can sign out in the App at any time. You can also revoke SynthFit’s access at Google Account → Third-party access

Google API Services User Data Policy

SynthFit’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. (policy).

Children

SynthFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SynthFit uses Google user data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use of optional Google features after a material change means you accept the updated policy for those features.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be emailed to syntithenai@gmail.com. For Google Account privacy controls, use Google’s account settings linked above.

Delete your SynthFit data

SynthFit — developer contact: syntithenai@gmail.com

SynthFit does not run its own user-account servers. Optional Google Sign-In uses your Google Account. Practice data is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in a YogApp folder in your Google Drive.

How to delete your data

1. Clear data in the app (recommended)

Open Settings and use Clear my data. Choose which categories to remove (sessions, tracking, routines, preferences, heart rate & band data, food diary). When you are signed in and online, Drive-backed categories are blanked on Google Drive as well.

2. Uninstall or clear app storage

On Android: Settings → Apps → SynthFit → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, and/or uninstall the app to remove remaining local data. On the website, clear this site’s data in your browser.

3. Delete Drive files

In Google Drive, delete the YogApp folder and any SynthFit sync files the app created under your account.

4. Revoke Google access

Google Account → Security → Third-party access → remove SynthFit / the OAuth app. Open Google third-party access

5. Questions

Email syntithenai@gmail.com with subject “SynthFit data deletion”. We do not hold a separate copy of your practice data on our servers; we can help confirm the steps above. (syntithenai@gmail.com)

What is deleted

  • Local preferences, practice history, streaks, custom routines, and optional wearable history stored in the app (for the categories you clear)
  • Sync files you delete from your YogApp Drive folder (or that Clear my data blanks while signed in)
  • App access to your Google Account after you revoke it

What may remain

  • Anything you leave in Google Drive until you delete it
  • Google’s own records of Sign-In / Drive activity under Google’s policies
  • Anonymous website visit counts (GoatCounter), which are not tied to your Google account

Retention

Local data lasts until you clear it or uninstall. Drive files last until you delete them or revoke access and remove them. We do not retain a separate account database.

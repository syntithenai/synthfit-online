Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SynthFit (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the SynthFit web and Android applications. SynthFit is a gym and personal-training app with optional wearables that works offline by default.

Synth Suite is the family that includes SynthYoga, SynthFit, Synth Flowarts, SynthHealth and Synth Workspace. This page is the shared privacy policy for that family; the Synth Workspace sections are marked below, and the rest describes SynthFit, whose website hosts this page.

Summary

Practice data stays on your device unless you choose optional Google Sign-In to sync it into files in your own Google Drive (a YogApp folder). Optional teaching features (teacher listings, blogs, coaching relationships, class bookings and studio clients, and purchase entitlements) use our suite server — see the sections below. Sign-in and Drive access are provided by Google; Google’s handling of your Google Account is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. On the website we keep a simple anonymous tally of which pages were visited (see below). (Google Privacy Policy).

Visitor counts

The SynthFit website uses GoatCounter to count visits to pages such as Routines, Learn, Privacy, and Settings. Counts are anonymous aggregates (no cookies, no accounts, no advertising profiles). GoatCounter’s own practices are described in their privacy policy. (GoatCounter,privacy policy).

Information we do not collect

We do not use advertising networks or tracking pixels. We do not run a general SynthFit database of your practice history: that stays on your device and, if you sync, in your own Google Drive. Optional teaching and booking features do store the listing, relationship, booking and entitlement records described below.

Community teachers and blogs

If you register as a teacher, publish a community blog post, coach students, or run class bookings while signed in, SynthFit stores the related records on our suite server (Cloud Run / Firestore) so those features can run. Practice history still stays on your device and Drive.

  • Teacher listings may include your Google sign-in email (used as the listing key), display name, short blurb, profile photo, website URL, optional contact email or phone, country/region, YouTube links, and approval or ban status. Blog posts include title, summary, body, image, author email, publish status, and moderation flags. Class bookings and studio clients, coaching relationship records, and purchase entitlements are described in the sections further down this page.
  • We use this information to show approved teachers and published posts, run the booking desk and coaching registry, let you edit your own content, and let site admins moderate listings and posts. Ban reasons are shown to the banned teacher in the App.
  • Community teacher, blog, booking and coaching records remain until you delete your account or ask for deletion (or an admin deletes them), subject to the retention rules in the Teacher and studio policy. Practice data in Google Drive is separate and is not copied into the community store.

Information stored on your device

To provide the App’s features, SynthFit stores data locally on your device (for example in the browser’s IndexedDB or the Android app’s local storage), including:

  • Preferences (hold times, music, reminders, accessibility options, and optional body profile details such as date of birth, weight, and height)
  • Practice session history and streak statistics
  • Custom routines you create
  • Optional wearable history (heart rate, steps, sleep, recovery metrics, blood pressure, SpO₂, weight, body fat) from Bluetooth devices you link, and optional Health Connect import/export on Android
  • If you connect a heart-rate device or BrainLink headset, a timeline of heart rate, calm and focus for each workout, plus BrainLink mental-state summaries and wearable-guided results, kept on this device only (never synced to Drive, and never sent to any server)
  • Optional food diary entries and meal photos (synced to yogapp-food.json in your Drive when signed in)

This data remains on your device (and in your Drive sync files when enabled). Coaching health snapshots are not stored by SynthFit; trainers only read a Drive file you share. You can remove local data by clearing the App’s site data in your browser, or by clearing/uninstalling the Android app.

Android Health Connect

On Android, you can optionally enable Health Connect sync. When enabled, SynthFit may read steps, workouts, sleep, historical heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen (SpO₂), weight, and body fat that other apps have written to Health Connect on your device, and may write yoga sessions, bike rides, day step totals from a linked Hume Band, cuff blood-pressure readings, and band SpO₂ readings back to Health Connect.

Health Connect sharing is on-device and user-controlled: other apps see data only if you grant them Health Connect permissions. SynthFit does not upload Health Connect data to its own servers. You can disable sync in Wearable settings and revoke permissions in Android Settings → Health Connect.

Health Connect does not provide live heart rate during practice. Live BPM still uses Bluetooth Low Energy.

Google Sign-In and Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, SynthFit uses Google Identity Services / OAuth to request the following scopes:

  • openid, email, and profile — so we can show your name, email address, and profile picture in the App’s profile UI
  • drive.file — so the App can create and update sync files it creates in your Google Drive (typically under a YogApp folder; not your whole Drive)
  • youtube.readonly — optional; requested only when you connect YouTube playlists for background music, so the App can list playlists you own and their videos (it does not modify your YouTube account)
  • calendar.readonly — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets the Schedule view read the Google Calendar events you choose to show next to your scheduled tasks. Events are read only for the date range you are viewing and are never modified, deleted or shared
  • calendar.events — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets you create, edit and delete events on your own Google calendars from the Schedule view, including when you ask the in-app agent to make the change. The app never changes calendar settings, sharing or access permissions
  • calendar.app.created — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets the app mirror its workspace schedule into a separate calendar that the app creates and owns in your account. The app only manages calendars it created
  • drive.appdata — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets the Settings backup keep a backup in the app’s own hidden data folder in your Google Drive. That folder is not listed in your Drive, and other apps cannot read it
  • contacts — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets the Contacts tab read your saved Google contacts and create, edit and delete contacts on your behalf so changes made in the app stay in step with your Google Contacts. The app only acts as you, on your own contacts
  • contacts.other.readonly — optional, and only in Synth Workspace; lets the Contacts tab read Google’s “Other contacts” (people you have emailed) so they appear in search and can be de-duplicated. This scope is read-only because Google does not allow editing Other contacts; an Other contact is copied into My Contacts only when you explicitly edit it

What Google user data we access

  • Basic profile information from Google (name, email, and profile picture URL) via Google’s userinfo endpoint
  • JSON sync files in a YogApp folder in your Drive containing your preferences, session history, stats, custom routines, optional food diary entries, and optional Hume vitals data. When you opt in to coaching health sharing, a separate dedicated health-share file may be shared with your trainer’s Google account as a reader only.
  • When you opt in: titles and video IDs of YouTube playlists you own, used only to play background music during practice
  • When you connect Google Contacts in Synth Workspace: the contact fields described under “Synth Workspace: Contacts”, used only to display and manage your own address book

How we use Google user data

We use this information only to:

  • Identify you in the App while you are signed in
  • Sync your SynthFit practice data across devices using files the App creates in your Google Drive
  • Play videos from YouTube playlists you choose as optional practice background music (via YouTube’s embed player)

We do not use Google user data for advertising, selling data, credit decisions, or training generalized AI/ML models. SynthFit does not store coaching health payloads on its own servers.

How we store Google user data

  • Profile display information (name, email, picture URL) is kept in memory for the current session while you are signed in
  • Practice data is stored on your device and, when sync is enabled, written only to App-created files in your Google Drive under your Google Account (in the YogApp folder)
  • Access tokens from Google are held in memory for API calls. If Google Sign-In session renewal is enabled, an encrypted refresh handle may be stored on our suite server so the app can renew access without repeated popups; it is not used to read your Drive files for any purpose other than the sync and coaching features you use.

Sharing, transfer, and disclosure

SynthFit does not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties. Sync writes go only to Google Drive APIs acting on your account. Optional coaching health sharing uses Google Drive file permissions you grant to a trainer’s Google email. Optional Android Health Connect read/write stays on your device and is shared only with other apps you authorize in Health Connect. Google may process data according to its own policies when you use Google Sign-In and Drive.

Data protection

Communication with Google APIs and our suite server uses HTTPS. Local practice data is stored in your device’s browser or app storage. Community teacher and blog content you submit is stored on SynthFit’s community service for moderation and public display when approved.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you clear App/site storage or uninstall the app
  • Drive sync files remain in your Google Account until you delete the YogApp folder/files (or revoke access via Google Account settings)
  • Optional teacher listings, blog posts, booking and coaching records remain on the suite server until deleted by you (where the App allows), by closing a studio after its grace period, by deleting your account, or by an admin — or until you contact us to request removal
  • You can sign out in the App at any time. You can also revoke SynthFit’s access at Google Account → Third-party accessGoogle Account → Third-party access

Synth Workspace: Schedule and Drive backup

Synth Workspace reads calendar events only to build the Schedule view you asked for, and only for the range on screen. The Drive backup stores the app’s own settings file so you can restore it in another session; it is not used for anything else.

Calendar events are not stored on our servers. Synth Workspace keeps only an encrypted OAuth refresh token so it can read your calendar again after it expires. The Drive backup lives in the hidden app data folder in your Google Drive, not on our servers.

Disconnecting the calendar in Synth Workspace — or revoking access in your Google Account — deletes the encrypted refresh token. The Drive backup remains until you delete it in Settings or revoke access.

Synth Workspace: Contacts

Connecting Google Contacts is optional. With your consent, Synth Workspace reads the contacts saved in your Google Account (“My Contacts”) and the people Google lists under “Other contacts”, and keeps a local copy in the workspace so you can search and manage them. When you create, edit or delete a contact in the workspace, the change is written back to your own Google Contacts on the next sync. Other contacts are read-only: Google does not allow them to be edited, so one is copied into My Contacts only when you explicitly edit it.

What Contacts data we access. Only the contact fields the feature displays and edits: display and given/family names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, website URLs, organization and job title, notes, birthdays, labels, photos, and the IDs and version tags Google uses to detect changes. The app does not read contacts from any other account, does not contact people on your behalf, and does not use contact data for advertising or to train AI models.

How we use Contacts data. To show, search and manage your address book in the Contacts tab; to keep in-app changes in step with your Google Contacts; and to let the optional in-app agent find a contact you ask about. The agent can change a contact only after you approve the change.

How we store Contacts data. A local copy of the contacts lives in the workspace’s app storage (the self-hosted server’s database, or the browser’s local storage in the browser-only version). Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored, either on the server or in the browser’s encrypted vault. If Google Contacts sync is not connected but the Drive backup is enabled, the workspace writes an encrypted backup of the local address book to the app’s hidden data folder in your Google Drive, so it can be restored on another session; that file is never stored on our servers.

Retention and deletion. Contacts stay in the workspace until you delete them. Deleting a contact that came from Google also deletes it in your Google Contacts on the next sync. Disconnecting Google Contacts, or revoking access in your Google Account, deletes the stored tokens but keeps the contacts already in the workspace. Clearing site/app data removes the browser-local copy. The optional encrypted Drive backup remains until you delete it in Settings or revoke Drive access.

Sharing. Synth Workspace does not sell, rent or transfer Contacts data to third parties. Contact data is sent only to the Google People API acting on your own account, and it is not shared with any advertising, analytics or AI-training service.

Our suite server

Optional teaching and booking features use our Cloud Run community service (Firestore). That covers teacher listings and blogs, coaching relationship records, purchase entitlements and payment state, class bookings and studio client data, bug reports, and Google Sign-In session handles so the apps can renew access without repeated popups. Practice history, custom routines and wearable timelines stay on your device and in your own Google Drive or iCloud.

Coaching with a teacher

When you coach with a teacher, assignments and recommendations are emailed. Your practice history stays on your device and in your own Google Drive or iCloud — it is not shared with the teacher through the app.

Our server keeps a small record of each coaching relationship so both sides can find each other: the two account ids, the app, the status, display names, contact email, the billing choice and payment state. Assignment emails may hold a short-lived stub of the content until you claim it (at most seven days). Recommendation notes are included in the email itself.

So the apps can notice changes without asking our server repeatedly, our publishing Google account shares one small file per coaching relationship with the teacher and student through Google Drive. It holds only the relationship id, its status, the payment status and when it last changed, and is deleted 30 days after the relationship ends.

Teacher listings and blogs

Teachers who list themselves publish the name, photo, description, contact details, location and optional YouTube links they enter, separately in each app. Blog posts they submit are stored on our community service for moderation and, when approved, shown publicly. Listings may be reviewed automatically or by a person; ban or rejection reasons can be shown to the teacher in the app.

Class bookings and studio clients

When you book a class, join a waitlist or buy a pass or membership on a teacher’s booking page, we store your email, optional name and phone, bookings, waitlist places, passes, memberships, attendance, late cancels and no-shows, and any notes, tags, waiver or intake answers the studio collects. Guests prove their email with a one-time code (kept so you can book again for about 30 days). This data is shown to that studio’s owner and staff according to their role. If you later sign in with the same email, your bookings and passes appear in the app.

We send booking codes, confirmations (with a calendar file), session changes and cancellations by email on every plan. Reminders, waitlist offers and payment notices may go by email or in-app notification depending on the teacher’s plan and your reminder settings. You can turn studio reminders off in My bookings.

Studio staff

Studio owners can invite staff by email with a role (owner, manager, teacher or front desk). Staff see the studio’s clients and bookings according to that role. Staff logins are capped by the teacher’s plan.

Payments

Purchases in the app stores are processed by Apple or Google, and RevenueCat confirms them to us using an anonymous account id. Web purchases, teacher plans and student subscriptions to a teacher are processed by Stripe; teachers receive student and class payments through Stripe Connect as the merchant of record. We store whether a purchase or subscription is active, and related entitlement and fee state — never card details. On the free Starter teacher plan we may take a small platform fee on the teacher’s Stripe sales; paid teacher plans take none.

Children

SynthFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how SynthFit uses Google user data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use of optional Google features after a material change means you accept the updated policy for those features.
